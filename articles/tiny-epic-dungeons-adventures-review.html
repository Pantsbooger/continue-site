Tiny Epic Dungeons Adventures: my review so far, and Chapter One keeps beating me

Four attempts into the campaign's first chapter, solo, and I still can't say whether I'm losing to good design or my own rules mistakes.

Clarkey · 15 September 2026 · 3 min read

Image: Tycoon Games

I own every Tiny Epic game that's ever come out. They're small-box games that punch well above their size, and the original Tiny Epic Dungeons is one of my favourites of the whole line. So when Tiny Epic Dungeons Adventures turned up, I was straight in. This is where I've got to with it: I'm four attempts deep into the first chapter, I still haven't beaten it, and I genuinely don't know yet if I'm enjoying myself.

A dungeon that plays great solo

It's cooperative, so it supports solo play out of the box, my preferred way to play currently, rather than switching into some bolted-on single-player mode. You just control two heroes instead of one. You build the dungeon room by room on a seven by seven grid, working through goblins, then a couple of mini-bosses, then whatever big bad is waiting at the end, one you don't even find out until you get there. Loot and levelling happen along the way, dice decide the fights, and the whole thing runs on a shared timer that also ticks the enemies' abilities forward. I have never once beaten a final boss in this game. I still think it's brilliant.

What Adventures changes

Adventures keeps that same core system and even lets you blend it with your original Tiny Epic Dungeons content, but out of the box it swaps the procedural dungeon for eight curated chapters, each one sealed in its own envelope. You flip open a starter book, build the dungeon on a fixed layout instead of a random one, and work through that chapter's objectives, closer to a legacy game or Gloomhaven than the original's roguelike shuffle. Goblins are out, replaced by eight mobs per chapter plus a chapter-specific boss, and there are mini and larger expansions built to slot straight in.

Chapter One won't let me through

Every run goes the same way. Early on it's genuinely fun, I'm finding loot, things feel like they're going well, and then the mobs start piling up faster than I can deal with them, I get cornered, the timer runs out, and that's the game over screen. Four attempts, four losses. I can't tell yet whether that's the chapter working as intended or me getting a rule wrong somewhere, and I've still got seven more chapters to go even if I do get past this one. What I'm actually unsure about is how loot and character progression are meant to carry across all eight chapters, since each session already hands out a decent amount of gear on its own. Maybe there's a reset or a downgrade built in somewhere down the line. I haven't seen it yet.

Early loot, mounting pressure, cornered, dead. Four times in a row.

One correction while I'm here: the Tiny Epic line was originally put out by Gamelyn Games, who sold the brand on to Tycoon Games in 2025, so Tycoon are the ones behind Adventures. Worth crediting properly given how much time I've put into this series. I'm going to keep playing, get Chapter One sorted one way or another, and come back to this once I've seen more of the campaign.

Continue playing?

Maybe

Genuinely fun in bursts, but four straight losses to Chapter One means the jury's still out.

Written by Clarkey.
