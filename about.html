About Continue?

Continue? is for people who like games, books, telly and board games, but haven't got the spare hours to keep up with everything the internet insists is essential this week. The name is the idea: you don't need to be first, you just need to pick things back up when you've got the time.

Most posts here are researched and drafted with AI assistance and checked against real reporting before they go up, alongside pieces written directly by the site's editor. Every article names its sources, and images are credited to whoever actually made them.
