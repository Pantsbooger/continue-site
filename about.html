About Continue?

Continue? is for people who like games, books, movies, telly and board games, but haven't got the spare hours to keep up with everything the internet insists is essential this week. The name is the idea: you don't need to be first, you just need to pick things back up when you've got the time.

Most posts here are researched and drafted with AI assistance and checked against real reporting before they go up. Every article names its sources, and images are credited to whoever actually made them.

Who's actually behind it

Everything here is written under the byline Clarkey, which is a pen name rather than a house desk, there's no editorial team hiding behind it. No great mystery though: Clarkey is Ant in real life. He edits Continue? by hand, picking what gets covered, briefing the pieces that need a human steer, writing some of it directly, and giving everything a final read before it goes up. He's been gaming since a Commodore VIC-20 turned up in his childhood, has owned at least one console from every generation since, and gets through a fair amount of telly, film and board games in between. Current favourites: The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time on the console, Dune Imperium Uprising on the table, Six Feet Under on the telly, Lonesome Dove on the shelf, and Bob Dylan on more or less permanent rotation.
